This policy describes how {{ brand_name }} (“we”, “our”, “the platform”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at {{ web_url }}, our cloud API, and the POS Mobile Android application (together, the “Services”).

1. Who we are

{{ brand_name }} provides point-of-sale software for retail and small businesses. Each business (“tenant”) operates its own store data within the platform. Contact: {{ contact_email }}.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: business name, owner name, username, email, phone, and password (stored hashed).
  • Sales & operations: products, prices, stock, sales, payments, layby, quotations, withdrawals, and shift data entered in the POS.
  • Device & app data: app version, sync timestamps, and locally cached catalog/sales needed for offline operation.
  • Bluetooth: the mobile app may pair with receipt printers you choose; we do not collect Bluetooth device identifiers on our servers.
  • Payments & subscriptions: if you pay for a subscription, payment references may be processed by our payment partner; we do not store full card numbers.

3. How we use information

  • Provide POS, inventory, reporting, and sync between devices and the cloud.
  • Authenticate users and enforce role-based access (admin, supervisor, cashier).
  • Print receipts and generate quotations or reports you request.
  • Process subscription billing and send service-related notices.
  • Improve reliability, security, and support.

4. Offline mobile app & sync

The Android app can record sales while offline. Queued transactions are stored on your device and uploaded to our servers when internet is available, typically within three days. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. You should keep devices secure with a screen lock.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only:

  • With service providers that host our infrastructure (e.g. cloud hosting), under contract.
  • With payment processors when you pay a subscription.
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Store data is isolated per tenant; other businesses on the platform cannot access your data.

6. Retention

We retain account and transaction data while your subscription or account is active and as needed for legal, tax, and reporting obligations. You may request deletion of your tenant account by contacting us; some records may be retained where required by law.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed. Traffic uses TLS (HTTPS). Access is controlled by authentication and roles. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; use strong passwords and protect devices.

8. Your choices

  • Update business and store settings in the admin area.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion via {{ contact_email }}.
  • Uninstall the mobile app to remove local cached data from that device.

9. Children

The Services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 13 (or 16 where applicable).

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the Services after changes means you accept the updated policy.
